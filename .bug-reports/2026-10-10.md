## Bugs
### Low - `AnimalMinionProjectile`'s big-hop-vs-small-hop wall check reads the tile above the minion's own position, not the tile above the wall it detected ahead
- Location: `Content/Minions/AnimalMinionProjectile.cs:188-198` (`ApplyGravityAndGroundCollision`),
  compare `:216-228` (`IsWallAhead`)
- Description: `IsWallAhead()` correctly looks at `aheadTileX = Center.X + direction * (width/2+4)`
  (the tile *in front of* the minion, in its direction of travel) to decide whether to hop at all.
  But once `wallAhead` is true, the code that picks *how high* to hop uses
  `footTileX = (int)(Projectile.Center.X / 16f)` - the minion's own center column, not
  `aheadTileX`/`direction` - to check `WorldGen.SolidTile(footTileX, footTileY - 2)` for a tall
  obstacle. These are two different tile columns whenever the minion isn't already standing inside
  the wall's column (which is the common case - `IsWallAhead` is deliberately checking a few pixels
  ahead so the hop triggers before collision, not after).
- Failure scenario: a minion walking toward a 2+ tile tall wall will, on the tick it first detects
  `wallAhead`, check for extra height at its own current column (typically still open air) instead
  of the wall's column, finds nothing solid there, and picks the weak `-7f` hop instead of the
  `-10f` one a tall wall needs - so it bonks into the wall short, falls back, and has to re-trigger
  the hop decision on a later tick once it's shuffled close enough that its own center column
  happens to coincide with the wall. Purely a minor animation/pathing quality issue (minions do
  eventually get over most obstacles via repeated attempts and the owner-chase hop fallback a few
  lines below) - not a crash, desync, or stuck-forever case.
- Suggested fix: reuse the same `aheadTileX` computation `IsWallAhead` already made (e.g. have
  `IsWallAhead` return the column it checked, or recompute
  `(int)((Projectile.Center.X + direction * (Projectile.width / 2 + 4)) / 16f)` here too) instead of
  `Projectile.Center.X / 16f`, so the height probe inspects the same column the flatness probe did.
