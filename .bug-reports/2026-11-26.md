## Bugs

### Medium - Genjutsu puppet/fear control never applies gravity, so a grounded NPC floats instead of falling
- Location: Common/GlobalNPCs/GenjutsuGlobalNPC.cs:53-75 (`PuppetMovement`), 77-91 (`FleeMovement`)
- Description: `PreAI` returns `false` for both `GenjutsuControlDebuff` (puppet) and
  `GenjutsuFearDebuff` (flee), which skips the NPC's own `AI()` call entirely - that's where
  vanilla AI code normally does `npc.velocity.Y += npc.gravity` for any ground-type NPC (nothing
  in the Terraria/tModLoader update loop applies gravity to an NPC outside its own `AI()`).
  `PuppetMovement` builds `desiredVelocity` purely from the controlling player's
  up/down/left/right input, and `FleeMovement` builds it purely from the normalized
  away-from-caster vector; neither ever adds `npc.gravity`, and `Collision.TileCollision` only
  clips the velocity it's given against solid tiles - it does not add gravity itself. Nothing
  else in the file or in the three debuff classes (`GenjutsuControlDebuff.cs`,
  `GenjutsuFearDebuff.cs`) sets `npc.noGravity = true` to make this intentional the way
  `GenjutsuSleepDebuff`'s "freeze at zero velocity" case arguably is.
- Failure scenario: a player applies Genjutsu control or fear to any ordinarily ground-walking,
  non-flying NPC (e.g. a zombie) that is standing on a ledge, over a pit, or near lava. While
  puppeted, walking it off the ledge (or, under fear, it fleeing away from the caster across one)
  leaves it hanging in mid-air over open space for the rest of the debuff's duration instead of
  falling - a plainly broken-looking physics glitch, and in the fear case the player doesn't even
  need to do anything; the NPC flees itself into the floating state. It also lets a player
  shepherd an otherwise-grounded enemy across a chasm or over lava that it could never normally
  cross, which is a real (if minor) exploit, not just a cosmetic blemish.
- Suggested fix: add `npc.velocity.Y += npc.gravity;` before the `Collision.TileCollision` call
  in both `PuppetMovement` and `FleeMovement` for any NPC that doesn't already fly
  (`!npc.noGravity`), the same way `Content/Minions/AnimalMinionProjectile.cs`'s
  `ApplyGravityAndGroundCollision` already does for the mod's own ground-walking minions.
