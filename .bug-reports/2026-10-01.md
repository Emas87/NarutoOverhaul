## Bugs

### High - Fireball, Great Breakthrough, and Water Dragon projectiles never set `Projectile.DamageType`, so they get none of the player's Ninjutsu damage bonuses
- Location: `Content/Projectiles/FireballProjectile.cs:16-28` (`SetDefaults`),
  `Content/Projectiles/GreatBreakthroughProjectile.cs:11-22` (`SetDefaults`),
  `Content/Projectiles/WaterDragonProjectile.cs:24-36` (`SetDefaults`); the Items that fire them
  (`Content/Items/Weapons/Jutsu/FireballItem.cs:24`,
  `Content/Items/Weapons/Jutsu/GreatBreakthroughItem.cs:22`,
  `Content/Items/Weapons/Jutsu/WaterDragonItem.cs:21`) all correctly set
  `Item.DamageType = ModContent.GetInstance<NinjutsuDamageClass>()`, but that assignment is on the
  `Item`, not the `Projectile`, and tModLoader does not copy it across at spawn time.
- Description: Every other Ninjutsu-class player projectile in the mod
  (`RasenganProjectile.cs:50`, `ChidoriProjectile.cs:49`, `AshBoneProjectile.cs:21`) and every
  Genjutsu one (`GenjutsuSleepProjectile.cs:27`, `GenjutsuIllusionProjectile.cs:29`,
  `ShinraTenseiProjectile.cs:29`, `SusanooProjectile.cs:51`) explicitly sets
  `Projectile.DamageType = ModContent.GetInstance<...DamageClass>()` in `SetDefaults`, matching
  the vanilla-weapon control group checked above (`KunaiProjectile`/`FumaShurikenProjectile`/
  `ExplosiveKunaiProjectile`/`PaperBombProjectile`, all of which set
  `Projectile.DamageType = DamageClass.Throwing` independently of their Item). `FireballProjectile`,
  `GreatBreakthroughProjectile`, and `WaterDragonProjectile` are the only 3 player-facing combat
  projectiles in the mod that skip this, leaving `Projectile.DamageType` at its default
  (`DamageClass.Default`, unrelated to `NinjutsuDamageClass` and not covered by
  `CombatStyleDamageClass`'s inheritance override, which only grants Ninjutsu/Taijutsu/Genjutsu
  inheritance *from* Generic - not the other direction for Default).
- Failure scenario: A player who equips any Ninjutsu-class damage bonus - the full Ninjutsu
  armor set (`NinjutsuBodyItem`/`NinjutsuHelmetItem`/`NinjutsuLegsItem`, 5% each via
  `player.GetDamage(NinjutsuDamageClass)`), the Ninjutsu Focus Seal (+10%), or the Ninjutsu Emblem
  (+15%) - sees those bonuses silently apply to Rasengan, Chidori, and All-Killing Ash Bones, but
  *not* to Fireball, Great Breakthrough, or Water Dragon Jutsu, which keep dealing their flat base
  `Item.damage` regardless of how much Ninjutsu gear is equipped. There's no error or log output;
  it just reads as those 3 specific jutsu scaling worse than the rest of the kit as a player
  gears up, with nothing in the tooltip or code explaining why.
- Suggested fix: Add `Projectile.DamageType = ModContent.GetInstance<NinjutsuDamageClass>();` to
  each of the 3 `SetDefaults()` methods, matching the line already present in every sibling
  Ninjutsu/Genjutsu projectile.
