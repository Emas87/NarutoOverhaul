## Bugs
### Low - Town NPC walk-cycle arm swing is lopsided due to floor-division truncation
- Location: `tools/generate_town_npc_sprites.py:279-285` (`build_frames`, the walk-cycle loop)
- Description: `arm_l=-off // 2, arm_r=off // 2` uses Python's `//` (floor division), which rounds
  towards negative infinity rather than towards zero. For `off=1` this gives `arm_l=-1, arm_r=0`,
  but for the mirrored `off=-1` it gives `arm_l=0, arm_r=-1` - the two should be exact mirror images
  of each other (since `leg_l=off, leg_r=-off` *is* a correct, symmetric mirror two lines above) but
  the arm offsets are not: one arm swings 1px further than its counterpart depending on the sign of
  `off`. Verified by evaluating the actual `walk` list against both expressions - every `off` with
  `abs(off)==1` produces an asymmetric `(arm_l, arm_r)` pair instead of a `(x, -x)` mirror.
- Failure scenario: every one of the 4 procedurally-generated Town NPC sprite sheets
  (`KakashiNPC`/`ItachiNPC`/`GuySenseiNPC`/`ShinobiVendorNPC.png`) gets a walk cycle where one arm's
  swing amplitude is visibly uneven relative to the other across the 12-frame walk block (frames
  8-19 of 25) - a subtle but real asymmetry a player will notice as a slightly "off" gait, in a mod
  whose own header comment says every frame must "read as a plausible idle/walk variant."
- Suggested fix: use sign-aware integer division instead of floor division, e.g.
  `arm_l=-int(off / 2), arm_r=int(off / 2)` (Python's `int()` truncates towards zero, matching
  `leg_l`/`leg_r`'s exact-mirror symmetry), then re-run
  `tools/generate_town_npc_sprites.py` to regenerate the 4 affected sheets.

- Location: `Content/Items/Consumables/HakuSummonItem.cs:12-26`, `KaguyaSummonItem.cs:15-29`,
  `KakuzuSummonItem.cs:13-27`, `MadaraSummonItem.cs:13-27`, `OrochimaruSummonItem.cs:12-26`,
  `PainSummonItem.cs:15-29`, `TailedBeastSummonItem.cs:11-25` (`SetDefaults`), plus each file's
  matching `UseItem` a few lines below
- Current: all 7 `SetDefaults()` bodies are character-for-character identical (`Item.width = 32`,
  `height = 32`, `maxStack = 20`, `consumable = true`, `useStyle = ItemUseStyleID.HoldUp`,
  `useAnimation = useTime = 30`, `useTurn = true`, `UseSound = SoundID.Item44`,
  `value = Item.sellPrice(gold: 2)`, `rare = ItemRarityID.LightRed`), and all 7 `UseItem()` bodies
  share the same `if (player.whoAmI == Main.myPlayer) NPC.NewNPC(..., (int)player.Center.X,
  (int)player.Center.Y - 300, ModContent.NPCType<TBoss>()); return true;` shape, differing only in
  which boss type they spawn. This codebase already has the exact fix for this shape of duplication
  applied to two sibling item families - `Content/Items/Consumables/BossBagItem.cs` (shared base for
  the 7 boss-bag drops) and `NumberedChakraScrollItem.cs`/`NumberedStaminaScrollItem.cs` (shared
  bases for the 7+7 story scrolls) - but the boss summon items are the one remaining family that was
  never collapsed the same way.

  `BossBagItem`/`NumberedChakraScrollItem` idiom already in this codebase) that owns the shared
  `SetDefaults`/`UseItem` body, leaving each of the 7 concrete classes to just declare its
  `CanUseItem` gate, `AddRecipes()`, and any boss-specific flavor (e.g. `PainSummonItem`'s
  town-proximity check). Pure mechanical extraction - no behavior change.
